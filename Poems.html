Poems For You.

Now here is a page filled with poems that make me think of you.


__________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

"Embed your soul within my cells,

Like shooting stars

Surrending to earth,

a cosmic collision that was

Always destined to be.

For I long to feel you upon my craters;

I cannot outrun the desire,

to eclipse all that I am with your mind.

Pour your heart into me.

Let me witness your fire.

Let me survive your storm."




___________________________________________________________________________________

"I think when death comes for me,

It will trick me by calling my name

In her voice so I wont resist.

I'll run toward it,

Arms open.

Finally, home."




___________________________________________________________________________________

"In every version of my life, I'll be

Lost in your eyes, forever and

Of all choices I'll choose you. It's my

Vow to your soul because my

Every heartbeat whispers your name.


You're my calm, my chaos, And

Only you turn ordinary days into magic.

Until Forever, I'll keep loving you."




____________________________________________________________________________________

"If kisses were stars,

I'd Leave

Constellations on

Your skin

And save the moon

For your smile."




___________________________________________________________________________________

"If I am born

a thousand times

I supsect I shall spend

a thousand lifetimes

Finding my way

Back to you."




_____________________________________________________________________________________

"You are a thief and I am a beggar.

You have stolen everything from me.

The air from my lungs, the in my chest,

And all the thoughts in my head.

The entirety of what you have stolen is yours,

And I'm begging you to take more."



______________________________________________________________________________________

"Cut my skin open

Crawl along my spine

All the way up to the nape of my neck.

Make a home out of my ribcage.

Split my heart apart exactly

Into two pieces

And place your soul within,

Where my heart has always carved

The exact shape of yours.

One day when our shoulders brush

Even in a crowded place

I would whisper, "Oh you"."




_______________________________________________________________________________________

"Take your part of me

Until nothing is left

But devotion.


Without hesitation,

I will stand between you

and tragedy.


You are worth

Every war

Across every lifetime."




________________________________________________________________________________________

"Maybe I could give you my eyes,

just for once.

Maybe then you could see

how beautiful youve always looked

through my eyes,

As if God made my eyes

with the clay that was left

while creating you."




_________________________________________________________________________________________

"Loving you,

It's not a decision,

Nor a rational choice,

It's an inevitability,

A whisper etched within me,

A shiver that doesn't lie,

As if my heart

Had always been waiting for you."




__________________________________________________________________________________________

"Forget flowers.

Bring me your darkness.

I'll match you shadow for shadow,

Hunger for desperate hunger.


We'll burn together

Until theres nothing left

But ash, truth

And tangled limbs."




___________________________________________________________________________________________

"Even in silence

of sleep,

My mind hums

with the melody of

your name."




___________________________________________________________________________________________

"I could have chosen a thousand roads,

Met a thousand souls,

And still,

My heart would pause at your name,

As if it had always know

Where it wanted to belong."
